How to Use Moji

1

Type on Any Website

Moji plays instant, responsive sound effects as you type in Docs, Notion, Gmail, Slack, search bars, and text fields.

2

Choose from 10 Packs

Pick your switch vibe: Mechanical, Cute, Thock, Typewriter, Laser, Arcade, Coach, and more.

3

Built-in Practice Mode

Click "Practice Typing" to test your WPM, accuracy, and keystroke rhythm directly in the extension.

4

100% Private & Offline

Password fields are auto-muted. Keystrokes are never logged, sent, or saved anywhere.

Quick Tip: Chrome blocks audio on chrome:// pages & the Web Store. Refresh existing tabs after installing!
